## Rijkswaterstaat sector geometry and observations

The stored VTS sector geometries originate from the official Rijkswaterstaat vts-deelsector_v data. The READMEs identify the source as GDR/fis_vnds, polygon layer 67; the current published main overlay uses FeatureServer layer 67. The geometry remains subject to its source terms and is not relicensed by this repository's GPL. Live observations retrieved from Rijkswaterstaat, where included, are also external data.
